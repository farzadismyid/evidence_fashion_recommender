# Human evaluation

The final explanation study should use at least two raters where feasible. Review packs
must hide the grounding variant and randomize presentation order. Keep the blinded sheet
and its private method key separate until ratings are frozen.

Recommended fields:

- acceptability: `accept`, `minor_issue`, or `reject`
- issue label: candidate substitution, wrong item type, unsupported attribute,
  unsupported occasion, overly generic, awkward wording, citation artifact, or other
- faithfulness score: 1–5
- usefulness score: 1–5

Report sample size, rater instructions, disagreements, adjudication policy, and an
inter-rater agreement statistic. Do not describe an empty review template as completed
manual evaluation.
